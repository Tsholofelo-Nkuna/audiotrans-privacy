Privacy Policy for MP3 Audio to Text Converter

Application: MP3 Audio to Text Converter (AudioTrans.Desktop) | Effective Date: November 21, 2026

MP3 Audio to Text Converter ("the Application") is designed with user privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Application on Windows.

1. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, audio recordings, transcribed text, usage analytics, telemetry, or diagnostic data.

2. 100% Offline Local Processing

All audio-to-text transcription is performed entirely on your local Windows device using a bundled AI model:

  • No Cloud Uploads: Your MP3 audio files never leave your computer.
  • Local Text & Drafts: Your generated transcripts and edited drafts remain strictly on your device until you choose to copy or export them.
  • No Internet Required: The Application functions 100% offline and makes zero external network connections during transcription.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application does not connect to third-party cloud APIs, external AI servers, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

4. Data Security

Because all audio files and text remain strictly on your local computer, the security of your files is governed by your own Windows device security and local file permissions.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

If future updates introduce optional online or cloud-connected features, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes are released.
